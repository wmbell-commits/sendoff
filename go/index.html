STAGE 1 · KEEP GOING

Imagine it's 11:45 PM.

Wedding. Concert. Ball game. Birthday. Casino. Your favorite bar. The night is exploding, the crew is locked in, and nobody is ready for it to end.

“Let's keep it going.”
STAGE 2 · ROUND TWO

You're in deep now.

You already know this is becoming one of those nights everybody talks about tomorrow. Before the next stop or the afterparty, somebody slides the crew a Round Two.

Hydrate. Reset. Keep the memory going.
STAGE 3 · THE SENDOFF

Then you hear it: “Last call.”

You look at the crew. Everybody is thinking the same thing.

“Time to SendOff.”

The bartender hits the cooler. Everybody gets one. Bottles up. Pictures happen. People toast. The end of the night becomes its own ritual.
